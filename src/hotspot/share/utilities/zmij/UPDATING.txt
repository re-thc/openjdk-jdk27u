Use a clean upstream checkout at the revision in make/data/zmij/version.txt.
From the JDK repository root, run:

    python3 make/scripts/update-zmij.py /path/to/zmij --commit "$(sed -n '2p' make/data/zmij/version.txt)"

The updater applies java-format, shortest-only and integer-only patches from
make/data/zmij in that order with zero fuzz, then verifies the production bridges before installing headers and the MIT license. Keep local
changes in those patches rather than editing generated files.
